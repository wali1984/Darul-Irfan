## Elevator pitch

**Darul Irfan is the official app of Silsila Naqshbandia Owaisiah — a
best-in-class, fully-offline azan and prayer companion built around the complete,
triple-verified Holy Qur'an and the Silsila's own library of verified content.**
Accurate prayer times, Qibla, the complete Qur'an with English and Urdu
translation, a daily spiritual companion, the works catalogue of Hazrat Ameer
Muhammad Akram Awan (RA), audio bayans, online zikr and events — all reverent,
ad-free, and private by design.

*"Verily, in the remembrance of Allah do hearts find peace." — Qur'an 13:28*

## Audience segments

1. **Followers & friends of the Silsila** — mureeds and admirers of Silsila
   Naqshbandia Owaisiah who want an official, trustworthy home for the Sheikh's
   bayans, works, online zikr schedule, and Dar-ul-Irfan events in their pocket.
2. **Everyday Muslims who want accurate prayers, done right** — people seeking a
   dependable, offline, ad-free azan and Qibla app with a genuinely complete,
   verified Qur'an and daily spiritual nourishment — without ads, trackers, or
   accounts.
3. **Seekers & students of tasawwuf** — those exploring Islamic spirituality who
   value verbatim, sourced scholarship (never AI-paraphrased) and the ability to
   study the Qur'an by theme, linked to the Silsila's library.

## Key differentiators (vs. generic azan apps)

- **Official Naqshbandia Owaisiah companion** — not a clone; built with the
  Silsila's identity, content, bayans, zikr guidance, and Dar-ul-Irfan events.
- **The complete, verified Qur'an** — all 114 surahs / 6,236 ayat, Arabic in the
  Amiri Quran mushaf face, with Pickthall (English) and Jalandhry (Urdu),
  triple-verified error-free against multiple authoritative sources.
- **The Sheikh's works** — the catalogue of Hazrat Ameer Muhammad Akram Awan (RA)
  (Asrar-at-Tanzil, Akram-ut-Tafaseer, Akram-ut-Tarajum) and a 291-item library,
  with full verbatim articles and pages, included with the owner's permission.
- **Explore the Qur'an by topic** — themes cross-linked across ayat, the library,
  and bayans; a study experience commodity prayer apps don't offer.
- **Private by design** — no ads, no trackers, no accounts; location stays
  on-device and is used only for prayer times and Qibla. Core features work fully
  offline.
- **Verbatim, never paraphrased** — religious content is preserved exactly and
  sourced; no AI-generated rulings or summaries.
- **Serious prayer engine** — 12 calculation methods, Hanafi/Shafi Asr,
  high-latitude rules, per-prayer offsets, rolling azan notifications, a gentle
  (no-shame) tracker, and widgets.

## Boilerplate — long

> **Darul Irfan** is the official iOS companion of **Silsila Naqshbandia
> Owaisiah**, based at Dar-ul-Irfan, Munara, District Chakwal, Pakistan, under the
> guidance of the present Sheikh, **Hazrat Ameer Abdul Qadeer Awan (MZA)**, and
> honouring the works of the late **Hazrat Ameer Muhammad Akram Awan (RA)**.
>
> The app pairs a best-in-class prayer engine — 12 calculation methods, Hanafi
> and Shafi Asr, high-latitude rules, per-prayer offsets, rolling adhan alerts, a
> Qibla compass, a gentle prayer tracker, and Home/Lock Screen widgets, all
> computed on-device and working fully offline — with the complete Holy Qur'an:
> every one of the 114 surahs and 6,236 ayat, set in the Amiri Quran mushaf
> typeface, with the Pickthall English and Jalandhry Urdu translations,
> triple-verified error-free against multiple authoritative digitisations.
>
> Around this it offers a living daily companion (an ayah, du'a, name of Allah,
> dhikr and Aqwal-e-Sheikh each day, with share cards), the ability to explore
> the Qur'an by theme, the Naqshbandia Owaisiah library (verbatim articles and
> About pages, and the works catalogue of Hazrat Ameer Muhammad Akram Awan (RA)),
> audio bayans and the Al-Murshid archive with background playback and offline
> downloads, the verified Method of Zikr with the online zikr schedule and a
> tasbih, Dar-ul-Irfan events with reminders and directions, the 99 Names, du'as,
> and the Hijri calendar — in full English and Urdu with complete right-to-left
> support.
>
> Darul Irfan is free and private by design: no ads, no trackers, no accounts,
> and no data collection. Religious content is preserved verbatim and sourced from
> naqshbandiaowaisiah.org with the owner's permission; the Qur'an Arabic and the
> Pickthall translation are public domain.
