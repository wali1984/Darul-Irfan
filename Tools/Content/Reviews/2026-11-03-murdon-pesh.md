# مُردوں: pesh on the mim (2026-11-03)

Six ayat of the Urdu translation had the pesh typed on the ر ("مرُدوں"):
27:80, 36:12, 42:9, 46:33, 50:44 and 100:9. The Urdu narration found them,
because the voice was given the mark where the text put it.

The 2020 volume prints مُردوں with the pesh on the م. That was read at 500 dpi on
PDF pages 735 (27:80, line 13) and 1144 (100:9, line 7). The other four come from the same
typed source and the same slip, so all six are now "مُردوں". No other letter
changed. The seeds stay byte-identical on iOS and Android.
